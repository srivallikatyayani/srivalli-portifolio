    I like problems that sit between fields, and I like them most once they turn into something people can use.

    Most of my projects start as a question. Can a small factory see its own emissions? Can a fake brand page be caught before it fools someone? Can a textbook chapter on trees become something you click through? I study computer science, and I keep pulling in other subjects to answer questions like these: a minor in robotics, a soft spot for organic chemistry, and NPTEL courses in topics that have nothing to do with each other.

    Outside the code, I write stories and speak on stage. Both have taught me that an idea only counts once someone else understands it.
